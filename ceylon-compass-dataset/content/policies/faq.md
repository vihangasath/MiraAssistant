# Frequently asked questions

## Are your tours private?
Yes. Every tour is private for your group, with your own vehicle and guide.

## Can you cater for dietary needs?
Yes. Vegetarian, vegan and halal meals are available on request.

## Do you offer airport transfers on their own?
Yes, from Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB) to anywhere on the island, priced by our team on request.

## Can you change a tour?
Yes. Every pre-designed tour can be customised. Our team prices custom changes.
