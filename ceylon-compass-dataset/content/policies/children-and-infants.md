# Children and infants

- **Infants under 2** travel free. They share their parents' seat and bed.
- **Children aged 2 to 11** pay child entrance fees, half-price meals and an extra bed in their parents' room on stays.
- **Children 12 and over** pay adult rates.

This policy replaces any older family offers shown on individual tour pages.
