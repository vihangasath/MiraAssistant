# Cancellation policy

This policy applies to all bookings made from 1 November 2026. It replaces any cancellation terms shown on older pages.

## Day and city tours
Free cancellation up to 48 hours before the start. No refund after that.

## Multi-day tours and stays
- 30 days or more before the start: full refund minus a USD 50 admin fee.
- 8 to 29 days before the start: 50% refund.
- 7 days or less: no refund.
