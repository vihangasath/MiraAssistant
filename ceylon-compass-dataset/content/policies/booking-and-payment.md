# Booking and payment

## How to book
Book any published tour in a conversation with Mira or on its tour page. Prices are quoted per booking in US dollars and include everything listed under each tour's inclusions. Optional excursions are not included.

## Payment
- **Day and city tours:** pay the full amount when you book.
- **Multi-day tours and stays:** pay a 25% deposit to confirm. The balance is due 30 days before the start. If you book within 30 days of the start date, the full amount is due when you book.
- We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express through a secure payment link. We do not accept cryptocurrency.

## Hotels
Where a tour says "or similar", we confirm the exact hotel at booking. Requests for a specific alternative hotel are priced by our team.

## Group size
Private tours are for 1 to 20 travellers. For larger groups, contact our team.
