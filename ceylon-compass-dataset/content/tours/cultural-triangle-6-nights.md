# Cultural Triangle Sri Lanka Package

The ancient kingdoms in a week: Anuradhapura, Polonnaruwa, the Sigiriya rock fortress, the Dambulla cave temple and the last royal capital, Kandy.

### Day 1: Colombo to Anuradhapura

Drive north and visit Wilpattu's edge villages on the way.

- **Meals:** Dinner
- **Accommodation:** Anuradhapura Heritage Lodge or similar
- **Entrance tickets included:** None
- **Other inclusions:** Air-conditioned vehicle and English-speaking guide

### Day 2: Anuradhapura

The sacred city by bicycle: Sri Maha Bodhi, Ruwanwelisaya and the great dagobas.

- **Meals:** Breakfast, lunch, dinner
- **Accommodation:** Anuradhapura Heritage Lodge or similar
- **Entrance tickets included:** Anuradhapura sacred city
- **Other inclusions:** Air-conditioned vehicle and English-speaking guide

### Day 3: Anuradhapura to Sigiriya

Mihintale on the way, afternoon village tour.

- **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner
- **Accommodation:** Sigiriya Village or similar
- **Entrance tickets included:** Mihintale, village tour
- **Other inclusions:** Air-conditioned vehicle and English-speaking guide

### Day 4: Sigiriya and Polonnaruwa

Sigiriya at sunrise, Polonnaruwa in the afternoon.

- **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner
- **Accommodation:** Sigiriya Village or similar
- **Entrance tickets included:** Sigiriya, Polonnaruwa
- **Other inclusions:** Air-conditioned vehicle and English-speaking guide

### Day 5: Dambulla to Kandy

Dambulla cave temple and a spice garden, evening at the Temple of the Tooth.

- **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner
- **Accommodation:** Hotel Suisse, Hotel Topaz or similar
- **Entrance tickets included:** Dambulla, spice garden, Temple of the Tooth
- **Other inclusions:** Air-conditioned vehicle and English-speaking guide

### Day 6: Kandy

Peradeniya gardens and a Kandyan dance show.

- **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner
- **Accommodation:** Hotel Suisse, Hotel Topaz or similar
- **Entrance tickets included:** Peradeniya gardens, cultural show
- **Other inclusions:** Air-conditioned vehicle and English-speaking guide

### Day 7: Kandy to Colombo

Drive back to Colombo or the airport.

- **Meals:** Breakfast
- **Accommodation:** None
- **Entrance tickets included:** None
- **Other inclusions:** Air-conditioned vehicle and English-speaking guide


## Inclusions
- Accommodation as listed, or a hotel of the same class if it is unavailable (confirmed at booking)
- Meals as listed for each day
- Private air-conditioned vehicle throughout
- English-speaking guide
- Entrance tickets for the sites listed, except those marked *optional*
- Two 500 ml bottles of water per person per day
- 24-hour emergency line
