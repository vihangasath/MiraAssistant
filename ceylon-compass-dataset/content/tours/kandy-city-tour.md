# Kandy City Tour

A full day around Sri Lanka's last royal capital: Kandy Lake, the market, a gem museum, the Peradeniya botanical gardens and the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, ending with a Kandyan dance show.

## Inclusions
- Private vehicle and guide
- Entrance to the temple, Peradeniya gardens and the cultural show
- Lunch

## Good to know
- Visitors to the temple should wear light-coloured clothing covering shoulders and knees.
