# Whale and Dolphin Watching Tour in Mirissa

An early boat trip out of Mirissa harbour to look for blue whales, sperm whales and spinner dolphins, returning to your beach hotel by early afternoon.

## Inclusions
- Transfers from your beach hotel
- Shared whale-watching boat with life jackets
- Light breakfast on board and lunch on return

## Good to know
- The main season is November to April. Sightings are never guaranteed.
- Boats leave at 6:30 am. Seas can be rough; tell us about sea-sickness when you book.
