# Train and Day Tour from Kandy to Nuwara Eliya

Take the morning hill-country train from Kandy through misty tea estates, then explore Nuwara Eliya, the town known as "Little England".

## Tour details (full day)
Board the morning train at Kandy in second class, with open windows for the views. In Nuwara Eliya, see Victoria Park, the racecourse and Lake Gregory, and stop for high tea at a historic colonial hotel. Visit a tea estate and factory to try plucking and see how black tea is made. Drive back to Kandy for a late dinner.

## Inclusions
- Second-class train ticket, Kandy to Nanu Oya
- High tea
- Private vehicle and guide; pick-up and drop-off in Kandy
- Tea-factory entrance
