# Highlights of Sri Lanka

For first-time visitors who want to see a bit of everything: the Cultural Triangle, wild elephants, the hill country by train, the south coast and Colombo.

### Day 1: Airport to Negombo

Meet and greet at the airport and transfer to Negombo.

- **Meals:** None
- **Accommodation:** Suriya Resort, Amaluna or similar
- **Entrance tickets included:** None
- **Other inclusions:** Air-conditioned vehicle and English-speaking guide

### Day 2: Negombo to Sigiriya

Dambulla cave temple, then an evening jeep safari in Minneriya National Park.

- **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner
- **Accommodation:** Sigiriya Village, Oak Ray Elephant Lake or similar
- **Entrance tickets included:** Dambulla cave temple, Minneriya safari
- **Other inclusions:** Air-conditioned vehicle and English-speaking guide

### Day 3: Sigiriya and Polonnaruwa

Climb Sigiriya at sunrise, village tour with lunch on lotus leaves, afternoon at Polonnaruwa.

- **Meals:** Breakfast, lunch, dinner
- **Accommodation:** Sigiriya Village, Oak Ray Elephant Lake or similar
- **Entrance tickets included:** Sigiriya, village tour, Polonnaruwa
- **Other inclusions:** Air-conditioned vehicle and English-speaking guide

### Day 4: Sigiriya to Kandy

Spice garden, Kandy city tour, Temple of the Tooth and a cultural show.

- **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner
- **Accommodation:** Hotel Suisse, Hotel Topaz or similar
- **Entrance tickets included:** Spice garden, Temple of the Tooth, cultural show
- **Other inclusions:** Air-conditioned vehicle and English-speaking guide

### Day 5: Train to Nuwara Eliya

Scenic train from Kandy to Nanu Oya, tea factory visit, Nuwara Eliya city tour.

- **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner
- **Accommodation:** Heaven Seven or similar
- **Entrance tickets included:** Train ticket (2nd class), tea factory
- **Other inclusions:** Air-conditioned vehicle and English-speaking guide

### Day 6: Nuwara Eliya to the south-west coast

Long drive to the coast. *Optional:* white-water rafting or a jungle walk at Kitulgala on the way.

- **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner
- **Accommodation:** Hikka Tranz by Cinnamon, Avani Kalutara or similar
- **Entrance tickets included:** None
- **Other inclusions:** Air-conditioned vehicle and English-speaking guide

### Day 7: South coast

Madu River mangrove boat safari, turtle hatchery, stilt fishermen and Galle Fort.

- **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner
- **Accommodation:** Hikka Tranz by Cinnamon, Avani Kalutara or similar
- **Entrance tickets included:** Madu River safari, turtle hatchery, Galle Fort
- **Other inclusions:** Air-conditioned vehicle and English-speaking guide

### Day 8: Colombo and departure

Colombo city tour and shopping, then drop-off at the airport.

- **Meals:** Breakfast
- **Accommodation:** None
- **Entrance tickets included:** Colombo city tour
- **Other inclusions:** Air-conditioned vehicle and English-speaking guide
