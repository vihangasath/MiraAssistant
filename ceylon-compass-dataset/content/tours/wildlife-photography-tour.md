# Wildlife Photography Tour

For photographers: elephants at Udawalawe, leopards and sloth bears at Yala, and water birds at Bundala, with a naturalist who knows where the light is.

## Route
- Udawalawe (2 nights) – Udawalawe Safari Camp or similar. Two jeep safaris.
- Yala (4 nights) – Cinnamon Wild Yala or similar. Four Yala safaris and one at Bundala.

## Inclusions
- Accommodation as listed, or a hotel of the same class if it is unavailable (confirmed at booking)
- Meals as listed for each day
- Private air-conditioned vehicle throughout
- English-speaking guide
- Entrance tickets for the sites listed, except those marked *optional*
- Two 500 ml bottles of water per person per day
- 24-hour emergency line
