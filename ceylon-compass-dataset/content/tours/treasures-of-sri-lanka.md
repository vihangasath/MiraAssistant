# Treasures of Sri Lanka

Our grand tour: ancient cities, temples, tea hills, the hill-country train, a leopard safari and the southern coast in two unhurried weeks.

1. Negombo – Suriya Resort or similar
2. Anuradhapura – Anuradhapura Heritage Lodge or similar
3. Sigiriya (2 nights) – Sigiriya Village or similar
4. Kandy (2 nights) – Hotel Suisse or similar
5. Nuwara Eliya – Heritance Tea Factory (no substitute; this is a signature stay)
6. Ella (2 nights) – Ella Gap View Lodge or similar
7. Yala – Cinnamon Wild Yala or similar
8. Galle (2 nights) – Galle Fort Hotel or similar
9. Hikkaduwa – Hikka Tranz by Cinnamon or similar
