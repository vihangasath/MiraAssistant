# Sigiriya and Minneriya Day Tour from Kandy

A long, rewarding day from Kandy into the Cultural Triangle: climb the Sigiriya rock fortress in the morning, then look for wild elephants on an afternoon jeep safari in Minneriya National Park.

## Highlights
- Sigiriya's frescoes, mirror wall, Lion Gate and summit palace ruins
- Afternoon jeep safari in Minneriya National Park
- Lunch at a village restaurant near Sigiriya

## Inclusions
- Pick-up and drop-off at your Kandy hotel
- Private air-conditioned vehicle and English-speaking guide
- Sigiriya entrance and Minneriya park entrance with safari jeep
- Lunch and bottled water

## Good to know
- Pick-up at 5:30 am; back in Kandy around 7:30 pm.
- Sigiriya has about 1,200 steps.
