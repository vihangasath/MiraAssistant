# Kandy Heritage Stay

A short, unhurried stay in Kandy in a colonial-era hotel by the lake, with a city tour, the Temple of the Tooth and a cultural show.

**Family offer: kids under 12 stay free!**

### Day 1: Arrive in Kandy

Check in and walk around Kandy Lake. Evening visit to the Temple of the Tooth.

- **Meals:** Dinner
- **Accommodation:** Hotel Suisse or similar
- **Entrance tickets included:** Temple of the Tooth
- **Other inclusions:** Air-conditioned vehicle and English-speaking guide

### Day 2: Kandy city tour

Peradeniya gardens, the market and a gem museum. Kandyan dance show in the evening.

- **Meals:** Breakfast, lunch, dinner
- **Accommodation:** Hotel Suisse or similar
- **Entrance tickets included:** Peradeniya gardens, cultural show
- **Other inclusions:** Air-conditioned vehicle and English-speaking guide

### Day 3: Departure

Free morning, then drop-off anywhere in Kandy.

- **Meals:** Breakfast
- **Accommodation:** None
- **Entrance tickets included:** None
- **Other inclusions:** Air-conditioned vehicle and English-speaking guide


## Inclusions
- Accommodation as listed, or a hotel of the same class if it is unavailable (confirmed at booking)
- Meals as listed for each day
- Private air-conditioned vehicle throughout
- English-speaking guide
- Entrance tickets for the sites listed, except those marked *optional*
- Two 500 ml bottles of water per person per day
- 24-hour emergency line
