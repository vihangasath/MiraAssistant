# Instafamous Sri Lanka

Eleven days of the island's most photographed places: Sigiriya at sunrise, the Nine Arches Bridge, the Kandy to Ella train, Coconut Tree Hill in Mirissa (where blue whales can be seen all year round) and Galle Fort's lighthouse at dusk. We plan every stop around the best light.

## Route
Negombo, Sigiriya (2 nights), Kandy, Nuwara Eliya, Ella (2 nights), Mirissa (2 nights), Galle (2 nights).
