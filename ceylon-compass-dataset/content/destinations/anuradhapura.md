# Anuradhapura

Sri Lanka's first great capital, founded in the 4th century BCE. Its sacred city holds the Sri Maha Bodhi tree and vast dagobas such as Ruwanwelisaya. It is best explored by bicycle.

**Tours that visit:** Cultural Triangle Sri Lanka Package, Treasures of Sri Lanka.
