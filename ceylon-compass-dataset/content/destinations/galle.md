# Galle

Galle Fort is a walled 17th-century town on the south-west coast and a UNESCO World Heritage Site, full of boutique hotels, cafés, galleries and churches.

**Tours that visit:** Galle Fort Walking Tour, Highlights of Sri Lanka, Treasures of Sri Lanka.
