# Kandy

Kandy was the last royal capital of Sri Lanka. The Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic sits beside a calm lake ringed by hills. The Royal Botanical Gardens at Peradeniya are just outside the city.

**Tours that visit:** Kandy City Tour, Kandy Heritage Stay, Highlights of Sri Lanka, Cultural Triangle Sri Lanka Package, Treasures of Sri Lanka.
