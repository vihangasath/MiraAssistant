# Minneriya National Park

Minneriya is famous for "the gathering": in the dry season, roughly August to October, hundreds of elephants come to the shrinking reservoir to drink and graze. Safaris are by jeep in the late afternoon.

**Tours that visit:** Sigiriya and Minneriya Day Tour from Kandy, Highlights of Sri Lanka.
