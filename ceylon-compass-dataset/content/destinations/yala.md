# Yala National Park

Yala, in the dry south-east, has one of the highest densities of leopards in the world, as well as elephants, sloth bears, crocodiles and over 200 bird species. Safaris run at dawn and in the late afternoon.

**Tours that visit:** Wildlife Photography Tour, Treasures of Sri Lanka.
