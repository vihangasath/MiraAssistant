# Sigiriya

The rock fortress of Sigiriya rises about 200 metres above the plains of the Cultural Triangle. King Kashyapa built his palace on the summit in the 5th century; its frescoes and Lion Gate are among Sri Lanka's best-known sights. The neighbouring Dambulla cave temple is a short drive away.

**Tours that visit:** Sigiriya and Minneriya Day Tour from Kandy, Highlights of Sri Lanka, Cultural Triangle Sri Lanka Package, Treasures of Sri Lanka.
