# Nuwara Eliya

At about 1,900 metres, Nuwara Eliya is the heart of tea country, with a cool climate, colonial buildings, Lake Gregory and a golf course. Nights can be cold.

**Tours that visit:** Train and Day Tour from Kandy to Nuwara Eliya, Highlights of Sri Lanka, Treasures of Sri Lanka.
